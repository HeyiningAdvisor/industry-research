# Heyining Industry Research

Two industry judgment reports on China's supply-chain localization, hosted at [research.heyining.com.cn](https://research.heyining.com.cn).

## Reports

### Semiconductor

**Design crowded, materials scarce, advanced nodes constrained.** 199 listed companies, 8 scarce segments, 7 real substitution constraints.

- [中文](/semiconductor/zh/)
- [English](/semiconductor/)

### Photovoltaic

**Barriers everywhere, none is truly stuck.** 33 listed companies, six segments, zero structural constraints (a negative finding).

- [中文](/pv/zh/)
- [English](/pv/)

## Scope and disclaimer

Both reports measure the distribution of listed entities &mdash; not a &ldquo;localization rate.&rdquo; They are compiled from public information and company periodic reports and do not constitute investment advice.
